Frequently asked, before signing.

Q.What's your typical turnaround?

Depends on scope — a focused MVP runs 4–8 weeks, a security audit 1–2 weeks. You'll get a written timeline before anything starts, not an estimate that slips silently.

Q.Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. Several current systems (DMDK, JawiKids) are already under NDA — happy to sign yours before any discovery call.

Q.I only have an idea, not a spec. Can you still help?

Yes — discovery exists for exactly this. I'll turn your idea into a scoped spec before a single line of code gets written.

Q.Do you work with non-technical founders?

Often. I explain trade-offs in plain terms and put decisions in writing, so you're never dependent on remembering a verbal conversation.

Q.Are you open to international / remote-only clients?

Yes — based in Malaysia, working with clients remotely across time zones, with on-site availability for GLC and regulated-sector engagements within Malaysia.

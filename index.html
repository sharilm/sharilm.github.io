Frequently asked questions by business clients.

Q.What is your typical turnaround for building a business software solution?

Turnaround depends on scope — a focused MVP or custom internal tool runs 4–8 weeks, while a security or architecture audit takes 1–2 weeks. You get a written timeline and milestone plan before work commences, not an estimate that slips silently.

Q.Do you sign NDAs (Non-Disclosure Agreements)?

Yes. Several active client systems (e.g., JawiKids) are under NDA. I am happy to sign your standard NDA prior to our initial discovery discussion.

Q.I have a business idea or operational problem, but no technical spec. Can you help?

Yes — discovery is designed specifically for this. We translate your business goals and operational bottlenecks into a concrete technical specification and architecture before writing code.

Q.Do you work with non-technical founders and business owners?

Often. Technical trade-offs are explained in plain, pragmatic terms with decisions documented in writing, ensuring you maintain full clarity and strategic control over your product.

Q.Are you open to international / remote clients as well as local Malaysian businesses?

Yes — based in Malaysia, I work effectively with remote clients worldwide across time zones, with on-site availability for enterprise, GLC, and regulated-sector engagements within Malaysia.
